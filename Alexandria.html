Explore Alexandria

Discover the city where history meets the beauty of the Mediterranean Sea.

About Alexandria

Alexandria is the pearl of the Mediterranean, known for its ancient history, beautiful coastline and cultural landmarks. Explore historic sites, seaside views and experiences that make Alexandria a unique destination.

Top Attractions

Bibliotheca Alexandrina

A modern cultural landmark inspired by the ancient Library of Alexandria, featuring museums, exhibitions and a huge collection of books.

Qaitbay Citadel

A historic fortress built on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria, offering amazing views of the Mediterranean Sea.

Montaza Palace

A beautiful palace surrounded by gardens, known for its peaceful atmosphere and stunning sea views.
