Discover the city of ancient temples and the treasures of Egyptian civilization.

About Luxor

Luxor is one of the world's greatest open-air museums, where thousands of years of ancient Egyptian history come to life. Explore magnificent temples, royal tombs and monuments that tell the story of ancient Egypt.

Top Attractions

Karnak Temple

One of the largest ancient religious complexes in the world, famous for its huge columns and impressive ancient architecture.

Valley of the Kings

A historic site containing the tombs of ancient Egyptian pharaohs, including the famous tomb of Tutankhamun.

Luxor Temple

A beautiful ancient temple located on the east bank of the Nile, known for its statues and amazing Egyptian designs.
