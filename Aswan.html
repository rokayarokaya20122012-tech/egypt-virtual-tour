About Aswan

Aswan is one of Egypt's most peaceful cities, famous for the Nile River, beautiful islands, ancient temples and Nubian culture. It offers visitors breathtaking landscapes and unforgettable historical experiences.

Top Attractions

Philae Temple

A magnificent temple dedicated to the goddess Isis, located on Agilkia Island.

Abu Simbel Temples

Two giant rock temples built by Ramses II, considered among Egypt's greatest monuments.

Aswan High Dam

One of Egypt's greatest engineering achievements, controlling the Nile and creating Lake Nasser.
